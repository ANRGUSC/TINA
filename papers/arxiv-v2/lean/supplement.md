## C5 Quadratic cost and completing the square (C5/p1)

The paper gives no proof. Supplement, written for the formalization (disposition C5/d1): the first-order condition Q x* = b, the expansion of f around x*, and positive definiteness for uniqueness. The paper says only 'Completing the square gives'.

**Step 1 (supplied). First-order condition Q x* = b.** Since $x^\star=Q^{-1}b$ and $Q$ is invertible, $Qx^\star=b$.

- Lean: `TinaV2.C05.A1.s1_first_order` (proved)

**Step 2 (supplied). Expand the cost around x*.** Write $x=x^\star+h$. Because $Q$ is symmetric, $f(x^\star+h)=f(x^\star)+h^\top(Qx^\star-b)+\tfrac12h^\top Qh$, and the middle term vanishes by step 1. With $h=x-x^\star$ this is Eq. \eqref{eq:complete_square}.

- Lean: `TinaV2.C05.A1.s2_complete_square` (proved); uses C5/p1/s1

**Step 3 (supplied). x* is the unique minimizer.** If $x\ne x^\star$ then $(x-x^\star)^\top Q(x-x^\star)>0$ because $Q$ is positive definite, so $f(x)>f(x^\star)$ by step 2.

- Lean: `TinaV2.C05.A1.s3_unique_minimizer` (proved); uses C5/p1/s2
